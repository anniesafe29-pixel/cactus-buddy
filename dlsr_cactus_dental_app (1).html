DLSR Dental Protocol

Dual Loop Sensory Recalibration (DLSR) is an active cognitive-sensory distraction protocol developed specifically for young children (ages 3–5) undergoing high-fear dental interventions (such as local anesthetic infiltration).

Loop 1: Active Mimetic Feedback
By recording the child's voice and instantly echoing it with a playful pitch shift (+35%) and dance animation, the child's sensory and cognitive bandwidth is actively occupied. This prevents focus on syringes, needles, and clinic noises.
Loop 2: Autonomic Calming Recalibration
Clinical thesis data confirms active distraction significantly reduces physiological tachycardia (averaging from ~137 bpm down to ~119 bpm, p < 0.001). The rhythmic 4-4 second paced breathing cycle activates vagal nerve tone, accelerating heart rate recovery.

Chairside Best Practices:

  • Introduce Cactus Buddy in the waiting area for 2 minutes.
  • Mount tablet on the dental chair light or delivery arm.
  • Activate Loop 1 right as topical gel or local anesthesia is prepared.
  • Transition to Loop 2 (Calm Breath) immediately after needle withdrawal.
